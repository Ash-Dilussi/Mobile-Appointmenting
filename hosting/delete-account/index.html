Delete your Bookly account

Enter the email address used for your Bookly account. We will send a time-limited verification link to that address. Nothing is deleted until you open the link and confirm control of the account email.

What happens

  • Your account identity and membership profile are deleted.
  • A sole owner must delete the business in the app or keep the account.
  • Business deletion immediately removes its memberships and server data.
  • Offline device copies are erased at their next launch or authentication check.

Requests are completed as soon as verification succeeds and always within a disclosed maximum of 30 days. For a manually assisted owner transfer or deletion help, email bookly.support@gmail.com.
